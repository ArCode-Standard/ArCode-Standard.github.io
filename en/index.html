About us

One language, shared standards

The same scientific term is rendered differently across languages, which erodes trust between translators, developers, and researchers. We address that by building a central terminology reference that is source-documented, tied to stable identifiers, and designed to grow and stay current.

Every definition in the ArCode-Core dictionary is carried over from its original source, cited against that reference, and linked to related terms through stable identifiers such as ACS-CS-0001 — making technical text machine-verifiable and safe to translate.

Our vision

To become the trusted Arabic reference for terminology, open-source projects, and technical standards — in the Arab world and beyond.

Our mission

To develop open-source projects, create applicable standards, document technical knowledge in Arabic, and build tooling that serves developers and researchers.

Reliability first

We accept no definition without a source. Every term is tied to its original reference — an international standard or an official report — and citation is part of the product, not an optional extra.

The organisation's foundational repository. It defines a structured system for Arabic scientific and technical terminology, unifying modern knowledge in Arabic with global scientific compatibility through stable identifiers, source-documented definitions, and interlinked terms.

ArCode-BERT

In development

An Arabic language model specialised for technical language, initialised from existing Arabic pretrained models and further adapted to understand terminology and technical domains — bridging the ArCode-Core reference and Arabic natural-language processing.
